## A constrained source-allocation candidate — not a finding

One **candidate** exact physical allocation consistent with *all seven* observed totals is:

| 7 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| **Total** | **20** | **30** | **2** | **52** |

This corresponds to the historical `20 Mortar + 30 Carabineer + 2 Wall` hypothesis. In contrast to the current modeled Carabineer vector `[2,4,4,4,4,4,2]`, this candidate is `[0,6,6,6,6,6,0]`. The candidate requires **moving/redistributing kills**, not simply adding eight Town-Wall casualties: current two outer slots already each exceed observation by one. Even if wall attacks caused exactly one death at each edge, model Carabineer kills there must be reduced from 2 to 0, while each of the five central positions must rise from 4 to 6.

**Unverified:** The screenshot contains neither per-source kill logs nor enough wall-attack targeting data to prove this source allocation. In particular, no evidence yet establishes that all seven outer Carabineer hits inflict zero deaths while inner groups achieve six each, or that the wall strikes the two edges. Multiple mechanistic allocations could fit the same totals. `EXP-WALL-OFFENSE-NONE-001`, `EXP-SR-DISABLED-001`, `EXP-IR-DISABLED-001`, and `EXP-ACCURACY-CONCENTRATION-001` are current *model assumptions*, not proven game rules.

## Independent controls and next discriminating evidence

1. The same Crispi input already matches **five wall breaches at physical positions 2–6**, defending Mortars losing **10**, and Bombardier deaths **20 attacking / 30 defending**. Any future model change must retain these outcomes and both-side empty Flank and no defender First in Round 1.
2. The independent **REIKJAVIKAS** battle has **no Town Wall**, both sides Forge Lv5 and different AA targets; its defending Bombardier deaths match 30, but observed attacker Gyrocopter deaths are **26 versus modeled 30**. It is a cross-case regression, *not* direct proof of Crispi firearm/wall behavior.
3. To distinguish fire allocation hypotheses, seek a real-game screenshot with comparable **Hoplite HP/armor and Carabineer numbers but no active wall**, ideally from **more than one AM slot-occupancy pattern**. A separate wall battle with fewer defending fire units and a visible wall-first firing phase is necessary to infer wall source/target behavior.
4. Do **not** activate RR (between rounds) or guess SR/IR (same-round redistribution) because a pooled upper bound seems to fit. Model one hypothesized mechanism at a time on an experimental branch, document exact per-slot predictions for *both* cases, and retain strict UNKNOWN guardrails.
5. `tests/unit/test_crispi_round1_position_matrix.py` pins the current signed residual vector `[-1,2,2,2,2,2,-1]` as an **explicit model baseline**, not a permanent requirement to keep the engine wrong. Replace that diagnostic expectation only alongside a justified calibration, revised evidence report, unchanged independent regressions and new exact-head CI/Codex review.

**Conclusion:** The eight-death *net* discrepancy is a position-specific redistribution/target-selection and possibly wall-offense problem; it cannot be fixed by a flat +8 wall kill patch without worsening already-overcounted outer slots. The actual game's precise Carabineer and wall source allocation remains **unknown**.
