# Architecture

Scenario models unit-specific Workshop upgrades and side Forge; registry models domain, role/support effect, semantic class, native line, size, and per-weapon accuracy/ammo. Packaged mechanics, units, Workshop, Forge, support, formation, targeting, battle-end, and calibration data load through `importlib.resources`. Battlefield geometry is the single executable authority for slot space and deployment order; the formation profile explicitly references that authority. The pure planner validates and executes profile eligibility, fallback eligibility, and Heavy-before-Light/front-before-flank precedence, assigning runtime `Slot.line` without mutating native metadata. Art of Warfare target priority is a known runtime-line role contract, while fallback weapon selection remains independent and unknown. Battle-end holding also consumes runtime lines; legacy `battlefield_class` remains compatibility metadata. Mixed runtime lines are representable but mixed-unit Am and non-native fallback combat remain guarded. Multi-line `Wave` execution remains guarded as `UNKNOWN-MULTILINE-WAVE-COMBAT-SEMANTICS`. Round-start formation rebuild precedes RR.
