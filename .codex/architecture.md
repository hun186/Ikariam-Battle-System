# Architecture

Scenario models unit-specific Workshop upgrades and side Forge; registry models domain, role/support effect, semantic class, native line, size, and per-weapon accuracy/ammo. Packaged mechanics, units, Workshop, Forge, support, formation, targeting, battle-end, and calibration data load through `importlib.resources`. Battlefield geometry is the single executable authority for slot space and deployment order; the formation profile explicitly references that authority. The pure planner validates and executes profile eligibility, fallback eligibility, and Heavy-before-Light/front-before-flank precedence, assigning runtime `Slot.line` without mutating native metadata. The formation layer also exposes eligible runtime lines so safety checks consume the same native/fallback contract rather than duplicating it. Art of Warfare target priority is a known runtime-line role contract. The independent weapon-selection layer preserves primary weapons for native combat and selects the sole registry weapon for runtime-first Light Infantry; damage calculation consumes that selected weapon and reports its ID/trigger. Multi-weapon fallback remains unsupported. Mixed runtime lines are representable but mixed-unit Am remains guarded. Multi-line `Wave` execution remains guarded as `UNKNOWN-MULTILINE-WAVE-COMBAT-SEMANTICS`; wholly reserve-only waves eligible for an occupied runtime line are guarded as `UNKNOWN-CROSS-WAVE-FORMATION-REBUILD`. Round-start same-wave formation rebuild precedes RR.
